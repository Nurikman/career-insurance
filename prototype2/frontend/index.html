    Privacy

    Your data, your choice

    Version 2026-11-15 · plain-language summary.

    What we store

    • Account: name, surname, country, email, password (hashed — we never see it).
    • Career goal: current and target role, target salary, study time.
    • Reports: skills and experience extracted from your CV, risk score, salary range, roadmap and interview results — so you can see history and download PDFs.
    • CV text and “About you”: only if you opt in. Deleted automatically after 90 days, or immediately when you withdraw consent.
    • Feedback and usage events (e.g. “report generated”) to improve the product.

    Why

    Only to give you your career report and improve tirek. We never sell your data and never use it for hiring decisions or advertising.

    Who processes it

    Your CV and profile text are sent to OpenAI to generate the report (as a processor; OpenAI states that API data is not used for model training by default). Data is hosted on our server (Railway). Job data comes from public employer job boards.

    Who can see it

    Only you, and the tirek team for support and quality checks. Team access shows masked emails and names by default; opening full details of an account is logged.

    Your rights

    • Download all your data (Profile → Your data → Download).
    • Change or withdraw consent at any time.
    • Delete your account and everything linked to it — immediately and permanently.

    Questions: contact the tirek team. AI results are estimates for learning and planning, not financial, legal or hiring advice.
